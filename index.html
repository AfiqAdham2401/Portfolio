About Me

Hi, I'm Afiq.

I’m an Information Systems graduate specializing in Intelligent Systems Engineering, with a growing interest in AI, data, and building practical intelligent systems. I enjoy exploring how technology can be used to turn complex or messy problems into structured, useful solutions.

My professional experience has exposed me to different sides of this space. At Nexagate, I worked on AI development involving RAG and Agentic RAG pipelines, LLM evaluation, prompt engineering, document processing, and cybersecurity-focused AI applications. At Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), I work primarily within the Safety/OSHE environment, supporting organizational and training data activities including data cleaning and transformation, Python-based data matching and validation, ETL processes, and reporting through Power BI and PowerPoint.

This experience has given me a practical perspective on how data and technology can support real operational and workplace safety needs. It has also shaped my interest in building systems that connect AI with the data and processes that exist around it.

Outside of my professional roles, I enjoy building and experimenting with my own systems. One of my current projects, Losen: Unordentliche Daten, is a modular AI-assisted data processing pipeline that explores how users can interactively clean, transform, and prepare data before using AI to generate structured insights, reports, and narratives.

I’m particularly interested in the parts of a system that happen before and after the AI model itself — from data preparation and system architecture to how users interact with the final output.

I’ve also had experience working in administrative and operational roles, where I dealt with inventory tracking, documentation, filing, and day-to-day organizational processes. During this time, I proposed a centralized internal messaging system to address communication and information-management needs.

Although the system was not ultimately deployed due to project purpose and implementation constraints, the experience taught me an important part of building technology: a technically possible solution still needs to make sense within an organization’s actual needs, resources, and processes.

Outside of technology, I teach chess, working with beginners and helping them develop analytical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Teaching has also strengthened the way I communicate ideas and adapt my approach to different people.
